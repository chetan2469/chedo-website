Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Chedo Tech (chedo.in) runs a simple static website for a programming institute in Pune. This page explains, in plain language, how information is handled — which is very little.

What This Policy Covers

This policy describes how chedo.in ("Chedo Tech", "we", "us") handles information when you visit this website. It applies only to this website, not to any third-party platforms we may link to.

Information You Send Us

This is a static website. The enquiry form does not submit data to any server: it opens WhatsApp on your own device with your message pre-filled. Any information you choose to send us — by WhatsApp, phone, email, or in person — is used only to respond to your enquiry and to help you with course guidance.

Information We Collect Automatically

This website is served as plain static files and currently does not use analytics trackers, cookies or advertising scripts. Your hosting provider or network may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and requested pages) as part of normal operation.

Third-Party Services

The site may link to third-party services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps and other platforms. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. If a Google Maps embed is enabled on the contact page, Google may set cookies according to its own policy.

Children's Privacy

We offer a kids programming course. Parents or guardians make all enquiries and provide any information on behalf of children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Your Choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information you have shared with us, to correct it, or to ask that we delete it. Our contact details are listed on the contact page.

Changes to This Policy

If this website later adds analytics, third-party forms or new features, this page will be updated accordingly. The date below shows the latest revision.
